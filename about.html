About InfoLinky

InfoLinky is an independent digital learning and discovery project focused on useful tools, clear explanations and responsible recommendations.

Our purpose

InfoLinky is a digital discovery and learning website. Its goal is to make technology and online work topics easier to understand through clear explanations, useful directories and practical guides.

What we cover

  • AI tools and software discovery
  • Software comparisons and evaluation guidance
  • Affiliate marketing education and disclosures
  • Online earning skills and safety awareness
  • Website building and productivity resources

Editorial approach

We aim to distinguish educational information from advertising, link to official sources where possible and avoid guarantees about product results or income. Product details can change, so readers should verify important terms with providers.
