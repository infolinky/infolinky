InfoLinky Digital Growth Starter Guide

A practical educational introduction to AI tools, online skills, websites and responsible affiliate marketing.

1. Evaluate AI tools carefully

Start with the task you need to complete. Compare accuracy, data handling, language support, free-plan limits and cancellation terms. Verify important details on the provider's official website.

2. Build a digital skill

Choose one useful skill, practice with small projects and create samples that show what you can do. Freelancing and remote work require effort and do not guarantee income.

3. Create a useful website

Plan your audience, publish original helpful content, make navigation clear, optimize images and test on mobile. Keep contact and policy information accurate.

4. Understand affiliate marketing

Affiliate links may earn a commission after a qualifying action. Disclose the relationship clearly, describe limitations honestly and recommend only relevant products.

5. Stay safe online

Avoid guaranteed-income promises, advance-fee job offers and requests for passwords or one-time codes. Verify employers and payment terms before starting work.
