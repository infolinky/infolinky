Affiliate Marketing

Learn how affiliate marketing works, how to choose relevant programs and how to recommend products transparently and responsibly.

How affiliate marketing works

Affiliate marketing is a performance-based arrangement. A publisher shares a tracked link to a product or service. If a visitor makes a qualifying purchase or completes another required action, the publisher may receive a commission under the program's terms.

1. Choose a useful niche

Focus on a subject you understand and an audience whose problems you can help solve.

2. Create helpful content

Publish tutorials, comparisons and honest explanations—not copied promotional claims.

3. Disclose relationships

Make it clear when a link may earn a commission, before visitors decide to click.

Important practices

  • Read each affiliate program's eligibility, traffic and payout rules.
  • Do not buy through your own links unless the program explicitly permits it.
  • Never promise earnings or misrepresent a product.
  • Keep records of links, disclosures and program changes.
Affiliate income is not guaranteed. Results depend on audience needs, content quality, traffic, conversion rates and program terms.
